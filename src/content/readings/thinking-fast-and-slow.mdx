Kahneman's Nobel Prize-winning work on cognitive biases and decision-making. Essential reading for anyone who wants to understand how their mind works.

Key concepts:
- System 1 (fast, intuitive) vs System 2 (slow, analytical)
- Cognitive biases that affect our decisions
- Prospect theory and loss aversion
- The illusion of understanding

This book fundamentally changed how I approach decision-making and problem-solving.
