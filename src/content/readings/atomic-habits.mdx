A masterclass in habit formation. Clear breaks down the science of habits into actionable steps, showing how small changes compound into massive results.

Key insights:
- Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement
- Focus on systems over goals
- Make habits obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying
- Identity-based habits are the most powerful

The book transformed how I think about building systems and creating lasting change.
